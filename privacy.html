SpotSet — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

SpotSet is a client-management app for personal trainers. It is published by Illume. This policy explains what the app stores, where it is stored, and who can see it.

1. We do not run a server

SpotSet has no backend of ours. We operate no database, receive no copy of your records, and have no ability to read what you enter. There is no account to create with us and no sign-up.

2. What the app stores

Everything you type into SpotSet stays on your device, in the app's local storage:

  • Client records you create — name, phone number, and notes you choose to enter
  • Training sessions, attendance and packages
  • Fitness evaluations (measurements, strength results) and generated training programs
  • App preferences — language, theme

The app collects nothing automatically. It has no analytics, no advertising SDK, no tracking, no crash-reporting service, and no third-party libraries that phone home. It does not request your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files.

3. Optional sync — your own private storage

Sync is off until you turn it on. If you turn it on, you supply your own GitHub access token and the app writes your data to a private repository that you own and control, so the same records appear on your other devices. The token is stored only on your device and is sent only to GitHub. We never see the token or the data.

GitHub is then processing that data on your behalf, under GitHub's privacy statement. If you never enable sync, no data ever leaves your device.

4. Data about other people

A trainer using SpotSet enters details about their own clients. Those records belong to the trainer, who is responsible for having their clients' agreement and for handling the records lawfully. We are not a party to that relationship and hold no copy.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer your data to anyone, for any purpose. There is nothing to sell — we do not hold it.

6. Retention and deletion

Data lives on your device until you delete it. Deleting a record in the app removes it from the device, and from your synced repository on the next sync. Uninstalling the app removes all local data. If you enabled sync, you can delete the private repository from your GitHub account at any time to erase the synced copy.

7. Children

SpotSet is a professional tool for trainers and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect anything from children.

8. Security

Local data is protected by your device's own security. Synced data is transmitted over HTTPS to a private repository. Because we hold no copy and no key, we cannot restore data on your behalf — keep your device backups and your GitHub account secure.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version is posted at this same address with a new date at the top.
