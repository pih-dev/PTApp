SpotSet — How to delete your data

Last updated: 20 August 2026 · App: SpotSet by Illume

SpotSet keeps your records on your own device, and — only if you switch sync on — in a private repository on your own GitHub account. There is no account with us and no copy on our side, so you delete your data yourself, at any time, without asking anyone.

Delete one record

  1. Open SpotSet.
  2. Open the client, session, evaluation or program you want to remove.
  3. Choose Delete. Deleting a client also deletes that client's sessions, evaluations and programs.

The record is removed from the device immediately, and from your synced repository on the next sync.

Delete everything on the device

  1. Uninstall SpotSet, or clear the app's storage from Android Settings → Apps → SpotSet → Storage → Clear data.
  2. All client records, sessions, evaluations, programs and settings on that device are erased.

Delete the synced copy

If you turned sync on, your data also sits in a private repository you own on GitHub:

  1. Sign in to github.com with your own account.
  2. Open the private repository you connected to SpotSet.
  3. Settings → Delete this repository, or delete the data file inside it.
  4. In SpotSet, open General → Backup and remove the sync token so the app stops syncing.

What is deleted, and what is kept

Deleted: everything — client names and phone numbers, notes, sessions, attendance, packages, evaluations, generated programs, and your app settings. Nothing is retained after the steps above; there is no server-side copy and therefore no retention period.

Kept: nothing. We do not hold your data, and we do not receive a copy at any point.
