**Date:** 2026-08-22 · **Pierre's picks after two listening rounds.**

## The suite, final

**anthem · engine · pulse · orbit · cascade.** Engine and pulse carry the fixes (metallic disc
ticks and a clean tonal riser — the sandpaper is gone); cascade is in; arena, droplet and maqam

## The wall, randomized

Every showcase loop now deals a **fresh random 24 from all 340 movements** — and no movement ever
appears twice on screen at once, including through the slow crossfades, which always swap to a
movement nobody else is showing. Rotating cells still turn on precomputed frames.
