# SpotSet v2.28 — the opening SOUND, and the Play release

**Date:** 2026-08-22 · **Asked for by Pierre** (relayed: "deploy to the Play Store… I want to hear
them on the phone as well").

## The sound

The opening now has its audio — synthesised for the mark, not stock: a sub-bass swell you feel
first, a wide shimmer that reads as space, and one clean impact landing at the exact moment the
posture lines start to draw. 1.35 seconds, high fidelity, ends before the app does.

**It plays in the NATIVE app only.** Browsers refuse to play sound before you touch the page, so
the PWA opening stays visual; the Android build lifts that restriction at launch (that is what
native buys). True "Dolby Atmos" is a licensed cinema format no phone speaker reproduces — this is
the stereo craft that carries the same feel.

## The Play release

versionCode 4 / v2.28, built and verified, for the existing **Closed testing – Alpha** track. It
carries everything since the testers' v2.15.1: the whole design pass, all 340 movement figures with
gestures and colours, the facing-pair logo, the backdrop, the opening with sound, and the new app
icon. Testers update through Play once the release is live on the track.
