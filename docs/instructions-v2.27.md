## The opening

Launch the app and the mark performs, once: the two figures step in, the green and orange posture
lines **draw themselves**, the fault ring lands, the name rises — then the screen lifts into the
app. Under 1.5 seconds, any tap skips it, and anyone with reduced-motion switched on never sees it.

**The sound is deliberately not in this release.** Browsers refuse to play audio before the user
touches the page, and an opening sound that needs a tap first is not an opening sound. The hook is
written into the splash component; the THX/Atmos-style spatial hit plays from there the day the
native (Capacitor) shell lands.
