# Published Fitness Norms — Research Findings (provenance)

Compiled 2026-06-09 by a research subagent under strict "cite or say NO NORM" rules,
to back the PT-facing reference workbook (`PT-Eval-PUBLISHED-NORMS-Reference.xlsx`).
Every number below was read from the named source, not paraphrased from search snippets.
Two widely-circulated fake "ACSM" tables (Topend push-up, Topend pull-up/sit-and-reach
"personal experience") were caught and REJECTED — not used.

## Bottom line for the 5-test plan
- **Push-up** and **Sit-and-reach** — solid published age×sex norms. Usable now.
- **Bodyweight squat** — NO under-60 norm exists. Only the 30-sec **chair-stand** test (Rikli &
  Jones Senior Fitness Test) is normed, and only for **60+**. (It happens to be the only genuinely
  30-second test in the set.)
- **Pull-up** — NO reliable civilian age×sex norm; women's norms effectively absent. Military
  anchors only (USMC max 23). Recommend: individual progress metric, not population classification.
- **Lung capacity** — no norm until a measure is chosen. **Peak-flow meter (PEF)** = best cheap
  field option with real reference values (Nunn & Gregg 1989, by height+age+sex). Breath-hold and
  balloon are un-normed. Spirometer FVC (GLI-2012/NHANES) = gold standard but needs a device.

## Critical caveats baked into the workbook
1. **Protocol mismatch.** The PT said "per 30 seconds." Published push-up/squat norms are
   to-failure or 1-minute, never 30s (except the 60+ chair stand). A 30s result cannot be honestly
   scored against a to-failure chart.
2. **Age-band mismatch.** Almost every source uses **20–29 as one band** — none splits 18-24 from
   25-29. YMCA sit-and-reach is the closest (18-25 / 26-35). Decide a mapping rule rather than
   inventing a 25-29 cut.
3. **Sit-and-reach zero-point.** YMCA sets ruler zero at the 15-inch mark at the feet (values in
   INCHES). Different sources (BrianMac/Davis: 15 cm zero; Topend: at-toes cm) are NOT
   interchangeable — same client, different raw number. Pick one protocol.

  registry (cite ACSM directly, NOT Topend's "unknown sources" table).
