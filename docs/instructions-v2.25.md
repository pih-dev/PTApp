# SpotSet v2.25 — the design round, and the app's own mark

**Date:** 2026-08-22 · **Asked for by Pierre** ("we need to do a round on the extra high of the
design refinements… it can be much, much, much better"), plus the logo (B3) from the same brief.

---

## The logo is now a figure from the library

The header mark (and the sign-in screen's) is no longer the placeholder dumbbell: it is the
**deadlift hinge silhouette from the app's own figure library** — the same drawing style you see on
every movement sheet, frozen as the mark. The app's thesis — the right posture — is now its face.
Tapping it still opens the movement library.

A judging sheet with the other candidates (squat, overhead press, row, and a correct+fault pair
lockup for the big store icon) is with Pierre; the mark can be swapped in one line if he prefers
another.

## Home answers "who is next" first

The **Due for renewal** block moved below the upcoming sessions. Between sets, the question is the
next session — it is now the second thing on the screen instead of the fourth. Renewals are still
there, still on the accent, below the list.

## The week figure is tappable

Tap any day column in the Home week figure and Schedule opens **on that day**. The figure used to
show seven days and go nowhere.

## Every screen speaks the same language now

- Schedule, Clients and Sessions open with the same **bar** head the Home screen has — collar,
  label, shaft, count. The old boxed section titles are gone.
- The Schedule **week strip** lost its seven grey boxes: days stand on a bar shaft, the selected
  day lifts and underlines in chalk, the accent dot still means "has sessions".
- The session rows on Home (expanded) and Schedule are now literally **the same component**, so
  they can never drift apart again. Schedule rows also show the live-session accent bar now.
- **Every emoji in the interface is gone**, replaced by drawn marks: the booking success check, the
  cancel X, the delete bin, the session-type dots (the type's colour, as a swatch). WhatsApp
  message templates keep their emojis — those belong to the messages, not the interface.

## Booking form: the common case first

The **Repeat** toggle moved from the top of the booking form to just above the weekday chips it
unlocks. Booking one client one slot — the thing you do ten times a day — no longer starts by
walking past an option it never uses.

## General is tidier

Sections use the compact bar style, the order puts what you use above housekeeping (Reference →
Backup → WhatsApp templates → To-do), and the green/red notification banners now follow the skin.
