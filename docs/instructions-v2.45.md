# v2.45 — The Second Anatomy Pass: Mirrored Signs, the Frame, and the Floor (2026-08-24)

*(Backfilled 2026-08-24 by the publishing session — v2.45 shipped from the figures thread
without this file; release-hygiene rule 3. Full record: commit `65afb51`, `HANDOFF-figures.md`.)*

Pierre's ask, relayed via the PTApp session: anatomically impossible details, and body parts
drawn outside the frame.

## The unifying defect
**A head-at-−x prone body is a MIRRORED figure — every relative joint sign flips.** The 08-23
pass flipped the lying leg curl's shin but not its ankle (feet hooked toward the head), and
bird-dog's support leg still carried upright signs.

## Fourteen patterns fixed
- **knee-flexion** — feet mirrored correctly (no longer hooking toward the head).
- **bird-dog** — rebuilt: trunk slopes so the 300-unit arm and 187-unit thigh both reach the
  floor (closes the v2.44 known issue, item 1c).
- **dip** — re-authored: arms had pointed straight up with hands and bars clipped above the
  cell since authoring; now grips bars at hip height.
- **triceps-overhead** — forearm folds behind the head instead of 52° of elbow hyperextension.
- **hip machines** — ground on the support leg (the hip-extension rule).
- **push-up / plank / knee-tuck / rollout / body-saw / rotation / anti-rotation (Pallof) /
  lateral-raise / side plank** — recentred or re-grounded so nothing leaves the cell or sinks
  under the floor.

## Two new build gates
- **FRAME** — warns on any joint outside the cell.
- The **ROM** check skips lying bodies it cannot honestly judge — its warning list dropped from
  24 noise lines to the leg press's two deliberate ones.
