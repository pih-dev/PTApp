# App Name Research — Store Publishing (Stage 2)

**Date:** 2026-07-14 · **Requested by:** Pierre · **Status:** screened via web search; final verification steps listed at the bottom.

## Why this matters now
Stage 2 (Capacitor native + App Store / Play Store publishing, see `stage2-publishing-guide.md`)
needs a final app name. Apple enforces **name uniqueness across the whole App Store** at the
moment you create the app record in App Store Connect; Google Play allows duplicate names, so
the real risk there is trademark conflict, not registration. CLAUDE.md has flagged since v2.x:
"PTApp is a working title — need a unique name, not trademarked in fitness/trainer space."

## Candidates Pierre asked about

### PTApp — technically possible, but weak
- **No exact-match app found** on either store (closest: "PT Fitness App" — a Trainerize
  white-label — plus PT Platform, PT Distinction, My PT Hub, PTP).
- Problems even if it registers: the "PT…" prefix space is extremely crowded, so search
  discoverability is near zero; "PTApp" reads as a placeholder, and Apple review (Guideline
  2.3.7) discourages generic names. Registrable ≠ good brand.

### PTAssist — ❌ DEAD
- **"PT Assist" already exists on the App Store in exactly our category**: a personal-trainer
  business-management app by John Simmons (apps.apple.com id6502348152, "a comprehensive
  digital ally crafted to streamline every facet of your fitness business").
- Also "PTassistance" (iOS + Android, physiotherapy) and "PT Assistance" (Android, physio
  booking). Apple would reject the name as non-unique, and the identical-category clash is a
  trademark/passing-off risk. Do not pursue.

## Recommendation (in order)

1. **PTDesk** — keeps the PT identity (continuity with the working title, the PT himself, and
   the docs), and "desk" says exactly what the app is: the trainer's admin desk — clients,
   sessions, renewals, evaluations, programs. Short, pronounceable in Arabic (بي تي دسك),
   30-char-safe, and the name space around it is verified empty.
2. **PTRoster** — distinctive, zero collisions, "roster" = the client list. Slightly harder for
   non-English speakers.
3. **Sessionly** — softest and most brandable, but generic-SaaS flavored and says "sessions"
   only, not the full trainer-desk scope.

Fallback if all three somehow fail final verification: PTApp (registrable but weak — last resort).

## What "available" still requires before committing (screening ≠ guarantee)

1. **App Store Connect name reservation** — the authoritative uniqueness check happens when the
   app record is created (needs the Apple Developer account, $99/yr). Create the record with the
   chosen name EARLY — a reserved name is held for you.
2. **Play Console** — no uniqueness gate; just register it.
3. **Trademark screen** — search USPTO (tmsearch.uspto.gov), EUIPO, and WIPO Global Brand DB for
   the chosen name in Nice classes 9 (software), 41 (fitness training), 42 (SaaS). We screened
   the app stores only; a registered-but-unlaunched mark wouldn't show in store search.
4. **Domain check** — e.g. ptdesk.app / ptdesk.com — not verified in this pass; check at a
   registrar when the name is chosen.
5. **Socials/handle check** — optional, but do it the same day the name is chosen.
